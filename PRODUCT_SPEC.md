# Product

## Register
product

## Users
1. **Players**: Pickleball enthusiasts looking to seamlessly book courts, find open matches, join tournaments, and manage their player profile. 
2. **Facility Owners**: Pickleball club and facility operators needing robust tools to manage court availability, host tournaments, manage staff, and handle bookings and payments.

## Product Purpose
A premium, full-stack pickleball community and booking platform. It aims to eliminate friction in finding courts and connecting players while providing owners with a scalable, professional management dashboard. Success means players can book a court in seconds and owners have zero double-booking or scheduling errors.

## Brand Personality
"Apple-tier", premium, flawless, and modern. The tone is highly polished, professional yet dynamic, evoking confidence and high-end software craftsmanship.

## Anti-references
- Do NOT use generic white/gray cards or heavy iOS-style blurs. 
- Avoid glowing neon blobs.
- No generic, flat, template-like SaaS layouts.
- Avoid cluttered, unstructured data tables for owners.

## Design Principles
1. **Premium Precision**: Every interaction, spacing, and micro-animation should feel intentional and snappy.
2. **Focus on Flow**: The primary action on any screen should be instantly obvious, stripping away unnecessary visual noise.
3. **Consistent Vocabulary**: Stick to the unified "dark pill" aesthetic and consistent semantic state colors (emerald for success, red for destructive).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure readable contrast for all body text. Critical states (booking confirmed, match cancelled) must be distinctly visible with appropriate color contrast and icons, accommodating users looking at their phones under bright court lighting or in motion.
